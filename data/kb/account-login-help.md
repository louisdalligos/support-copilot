# Trouble signing in

- On the sign-in page, enter your email and select Email me a link. The link is valid for 15 minutes.
- Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.
- Orders placed as a guest are linked to your account automatically when you sign up with the same email.

If you no longer have access to the email on your account, contact us from your new address and include a recent order number so we can verify you.
