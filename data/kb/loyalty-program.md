# Loyalty program

- Earn 1 point for every $1 you spend on products. Shipping, taxes and gift cards do not earn points.
- Every 100 points is worth $5 off a future order.
- Points expire 12 months after your last order.
- If an order is refunded, the points earned on it are removed.

Points appear in your account once your order ships.
