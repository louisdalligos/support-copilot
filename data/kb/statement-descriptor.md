# A charge I don't recognize

Our charges appear on bank and card statements as:

- NWC*NORTHWIND COFFEE for orders
- NWC*COFFEE SUBSCRIPTION for subscription renewals

## Before you contact your bank

Many unrecognized charges turn out to be:

- A subscription renewal you forgot about
- An order placed by someone in your household using your card
- A gift order sent to someone else

## We can look it up

Email support@northwind-coffee.example with the date, the amount and the last 4 digits of the card. We will find the order and explain it, or refund it if it was not authorized. This is faster than a bank dispute. See Disputes and chargebacks.
