# Update the card on a subscription

1. Sign in and open Subscriptions.
2. Select Payment method, then Update card.
3. Enter the new card details and save.

The new card is used from the next renewal.

## If a renewal payment fails

We email you and retry the payment 3 times over 7 days. If all attempts fail, the subscription is paused until you update your card. No shipment is sent for a renewal that was not paid.
