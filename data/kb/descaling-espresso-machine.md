# Descaling your espresso machine

Descale every 2 to 3 months, or monthly if you have hard water. Scale buildup slows the water flow and can damage the heater, and damage from scale is not covered by the Equipment warranty.

1. Empty the water tank and fill it with descaling solution mixed as the label says.
2. Run half the tank through the group head and half through the steam wand.
3. Let the machine sit for 20 minutes.
4. Run the rest through, then rinse with 2 full tanks of clean water.

Do not use vinegar: it can damage seals.
