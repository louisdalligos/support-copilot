# Wholesale and cafe accounts

We supply cafes, offices and shops.

- Minimum order of $500.
- Approved accounts can pay on net 30 terms.
- Coffee is available in 5 lb bags.

To apply, email wholesale@northwind-coffee.example with your business name, location and expected monthly volume. Wholesale orders follow separate terms; the retail Refund policy does not apply to them.
