# Shipping policy

We ship from Portland, Oregon to all 50 US states.

| Method | Price | Delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $6.95, free on orders of $50 or more | 3 to 5 business days |
| Express | $14.95 | 1 to 2 business days |

Free shipping applies to the order subtotal after discounts and before tax.

## When orders ship

We roast Monday to Thursday. Orders placed before 1pm Pacific time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Tracking

You get a tracking email as soon as your order ships. See Track your order.

For orders outside the US, see International shipping.
