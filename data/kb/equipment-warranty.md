# Equipment warranty

All brewing equipment we sell is covered for 1 year from the delivery date against manufacturing defects.

## What is covered

Parts that fail in normal use, such as a grinder motor, a kettle heating element or a cracked carafe that was not dropped.

## What is not covered

- Damage from drops, misuse or modifications
- Scale buildup caused by not descaling
- Normal cosmetic wear

## How to make a claim

Email us your order number, a description of the problem and, if you can, a short video. We repair or replace the item. Within the first 30 days after delivery you can choose a refund instead, under our Refund policy.
