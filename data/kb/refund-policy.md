# Refund policy

This policy applies to orders delivered on or after March 1, 2026.

## Change of mind

You can return unopened coffee and unused equipment for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Opened bags of coffee cannot be returned for a change of mind, because we cannot resell them.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or a full refund, your choice.

Coffee that tastes stale or is otherwise defective is covered for 30 days after delivery.

Equipment that develops a defect more than 30 days after delivery is covered by our 1-year equipment warranty instead of a refund. See Equipment warranty.

## Items that cannot be refunded

- Gift cards
- Items marked Final Sale
- Shipping fees, unless the problem was our mistake

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once we issue a refund, it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank. We never refund more than you paid for the order.

## Subscriptions

A subscription renewal can be refunded in full if the renewal order has not shipped yet. See Subscription terms.
