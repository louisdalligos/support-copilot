# Roast levels explained

- Light roast: bright and acidic, with fruity or floral notes. Best for pour-over.
- Medium roast: balanced sweetness and body, with chocolate and caramel notes. Works with every brew method.
- Dark roast: bold, smoky and low in acidity. Good for espresso and with milk.

Caffeine differs very little between roast levels. Light roast has slightly more caffeine by weight.
