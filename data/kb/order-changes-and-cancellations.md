# Change or cancel an order

You can change or cancel an order until we start packing it, usually within 1 hour of placing it. Email us with your order number as soon as possible.

- We can change the shipping address, the grind, or the items in the order.
- A cancelled order is refunded in full to your original payment method.

Once an order is packed or shipped, it can no longer be changed or cancelled. You can return it instead, under our Refund policy.
