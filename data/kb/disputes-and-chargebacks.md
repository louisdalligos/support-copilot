# Disputes and chargebacks

If something is wrong with a charge, please contact us first. We usually resolve payment problems within 1 to 2 business days. A dispute through your bank can take up to 75 days.

## If you already filed a dispute

Let us know. If we resolve the problem directly, for example with a refund, tell your bank so it can close the dispute. Keeping a dispute open after you have been refunded can delay the case.

## What we do

When a dispute is opened, we send the bank the order details, delivery confirmation and our conversation with you.
