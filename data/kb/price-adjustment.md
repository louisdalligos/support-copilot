# Price adjustments

If we lower the price of an item within 7 days after you bought it, we refund the difference. Contact us with your order number.

- One price adjustment per order.
- Does not apply to flash sales, clearance items or discounts from promo codes.
- The difference goes back to your original payment method and takes 5 to 10 business days to appear.
