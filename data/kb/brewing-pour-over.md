# Brewing guide: pour-over

You need 20 g of medium-fine coffee, 320 g of water just off the boil (about 94°C or 200°F), a paper filter and a dripper.

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the water.
2. Add the coffee and pour 50 g of water to wet it. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Pour the rest of the water in slow circles until you reach 320 g.
4. Total brew time should be about 3 minutes.

Too fast and sour? Grind finer. Too slow and bitter? Grind coarser.
