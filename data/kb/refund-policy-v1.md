# Refund policy

This policy applied to orders delivered before March 1, 2026. It has been replaced.

## Returns

You can return any item within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bags of coffee can be returned within 14 days if at least half of the bag is left.

## Damaged items

Report damaged items within 7 days of delivery for a replacement.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Shipping fees are not refunded.
