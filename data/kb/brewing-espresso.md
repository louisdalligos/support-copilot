# Brewing guide: espresso

Start with 18 g of finely ground coffee for a double shot, aiming for about 36 g of espresso in 25 to 30 seconds.

- Shot runs too fast and tastes sour: grind finer.
- Shot runs too slow and tastes bitter: grind coarser.
- Change only one thing at a time.

Use coffee 7 to 21 days after its roast date for the best espresso. Very fresh coffee releases a lot of gas and can make shots uneven.
