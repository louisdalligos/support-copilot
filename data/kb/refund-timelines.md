# When will I get my refund?

We email you as soon as we issue a refund. After that, it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement. The exact timing depends on your bank.

- Partial refunds appear as a separate credit, not as a change to the original charge.
- Refunds to PayPal and Shop Pay usually appear faster, often within 3 business days.
- If a pending charge disappears instead of being refunded, that is normal: the bank released the hold.

## Still not there after 10 business days?

Contact your bank with the refund date and amount from our email. If the bank cannot find it, contact us and we will send you the refund reference number.
