# Gift cards

- Gift cards never expire.
- They cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash.
- They can be used on any product, including subscriptions.
- If a gift card code is lost, we can reissue it with proof of purchase.

Gift cards are delivered by email to the recipient on the date you choose.
