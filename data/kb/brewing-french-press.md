# Brewing guide: French press

You need 30 g of coarse coffee and 500 g of water just off the boil.

1. Add the coffee to the press and pour in all the water.
2. Stir gently and put the lid on without pressing.
3. Wait 4 minutes.
4. Press the plunger down slowly and serve right away, so the coffee does not keep brewing.

Sludge in your cup usually means the grind is too fine.
