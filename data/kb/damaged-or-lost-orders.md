# Damaged, lost or missing orders

## Damaged items

Tell us within 14 days of delivery and include a photo of the damage and the packaging. We send a replacement or a full refund, your choice.

## Lost in transit

If tracking has not updated for 7 days, or your order has not arrived 7 days after the expected delivery date, contact us. We file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement right away. You do not need to wait for the claim.

## Marked as delivered but missing

Check with neighbors and around your property, and wait 48 hours, since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still has not appeared, contact us and we will send a replacement. We can replace a missing delivery once per customer every 12 months.
