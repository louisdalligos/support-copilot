# Storing coffee

Every bag shows its roast date. Coffee tastes best within 4 weeks of that date.

- Keep the bag sealed, or use an airtight container.
- Store it somewhere cool and dark, away from the stove.
- Do not store opened coffee in the fridge: it absorbs moisture and smells.
- You can freeze unopened bags for up to 3 months. Thaw them fully before opening.

Our bags have a one-way valve that lets gas out without letting air in.
