# Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.

Your payment is charged when you place the order. Subscription renewals are charged on each renewal date.

Charges appear on your statement as NWC*NORTHWIND COFFEE for orders and NWC*COFFEE SUBSCRIPTION for subscription renewals.

We do not store full card numbers. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments.
