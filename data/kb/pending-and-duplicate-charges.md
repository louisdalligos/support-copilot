# Pending and duplicate charges

## Two charges for one order?

When you place an order, your bank may show a pending authorization. If a payment attempt failed and you tried again, you may see two pending amounts. We only collect payment once. The extra pending hold is released by your bank, usually within 3 to 7 business days, and is never taken from your account.

## Two completed charges

If your statement shows two completed (posted, not pending) charges for the same order, contact us with the order number. We check the payment record and refund the duplicate right away. The refund then takes 5 to 10 business days to appear.
