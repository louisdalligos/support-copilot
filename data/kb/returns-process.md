# How to return an item

1. Email support@northwind-coffee.example with your order number and the items you want to return.
2. Tell us why you are returning them. For damaged or wrong items, attach a photo.
3. We reply within 1 business day with next steps.

## Damaged or wrong items

We email you a prepaid return label. For damaged coffee you usually do not need to send it back: a photo is enough.

## Change of mind

Pack the unopened coffee or unused equipment in its original packaging and ship it to the address we send you. Return shipping is paid by you.

## When you get your money back

We issue the refund as soon as the carrier scans your return, or right away when no return is needed. The refund then takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement.

Returns are only accepted within the windows in our Refund policy.
