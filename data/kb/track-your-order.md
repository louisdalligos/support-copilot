# Track your order

When your order ships we email you a tracking link. You can also find it under Orders in your account.

## Tracking statuses

- Label created: the shipping label is printed and the carrier has not scanned the package yet. This can last up to 24 hours.
- In transit: the package is on its way.
- Out for delivery: it arrives today.
- Delivered: the carrier marked it delivered. If you cannot find it, see Damaged, lost or missing orders.

We ship with USPS and UPS.
