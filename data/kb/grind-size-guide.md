# Grind size guide

When you order, choose whole bean or one of our grinds:

| Grind | Texture | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coarse | Like sea salt | French press, cold brew |
| Medium | Like sand | Drip machines |
| Medium-fine | Like table salt | Pour-over |
| Fine | Like powdered sugar | Espresso |

Whole beans stay fresh longest. Ground coffee is best used within 2 weeks of opening.

If your coffee tastes bitter, try a coarser grind. If it tastes sour or weak, try a finer grind.
