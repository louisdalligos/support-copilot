# How our decaf is made

All our decaf coffees use the Swiss Water Process. Green coffee is soaked in water to remove caffeine, and the water is filtered through activated charcoal. No chemical solvents are used.

The process removes 99.9% of the caffeine. A cup of our decaf contains about 2 to 5 mg of caffeine, compared with about 95 mg in a regular cup.
