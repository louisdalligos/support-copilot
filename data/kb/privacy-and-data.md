# Your personal data

We use your name, email, address and order history to process orders and provide support. We never sell personal data.

You can ask us to:

- Send you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- Correct it
- Delete it

Email support@northwind-coffee.example from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days. We keep order records required for tax and accounting even after a deletion request.
