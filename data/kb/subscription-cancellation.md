# Cancel a subscription

You can cancel at any time, with no fees.

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Go to Subscriptions and choose the subscription.
3. Select Cancel subscription and confirm.

You can also email support@northwind-coffee.example and we will cancel it for you.

## Timing

Cancel before your next renewal date to avoid the next charge. If a renewal has already been charged but has not shipped yet, we refund it in full. If it has already shipped, that shipment is not refundable unless something is wrong with it.

Prefer a break instead? See Skip or pause a subscription.
