# Skip or pause a subscription

## Skip one delivery

In your account, open Subscriptions and select Skip next delivery. Your following delivery goes ahead as normal. Skip at least 1 day before the renewal date.

## Pause

You can pause a subscription for up to 3 months. Pick the date you want deliveries to start again. If you do nothing, the subscription restarts automatically on that date.

## Change coffee or frequency

You can change the coffee, the grind or the delivery frequency at any time before the next renewal date.
