# Grinder troubleshooting

## The grinder will not start

Check that the hopper is locked into place and the bin is fully inserted. The motor will not run unless both are seated.

## Grinding is slow or the motor sounds strained

The burrs may be clogged. Unplug the grinder, remove the upper burr and brush out the old grounds. Clean the burrs every 2 weeks with regular use.

## Grind size is uneven

Make sure the upper burr is seated correctly after cleaning. If the grind is still uneven, the burrs may be worn.

If the motor still fails, contact us: it may be covered by the Equipment warranty.
