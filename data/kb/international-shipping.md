# International shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia.

- Flat rate of $19.95 per order. Free shipping does not apply outside the US.
- Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.
- Import duties and taxes are charged by your country's customs and paid by you on delivery. We cannot refund them.
- Subscriptions are only available in the US.

If a package is held at customs for more than 14 days, contact us and we will help trace it.
