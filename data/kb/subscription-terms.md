# Subscription terms

## How it works

Choose a coffee and a delivery frequency of every 2, 4 or 6 weeks. Subscribers save 10% on every shipment and get free standard shipping.

## Billing

Each renewal is charged on its renewal date to the card saved on your subscription. It appears on your statement as NWC*COFFEE SUBSCRIPTION. We email a reminder 3 days before each renewal.

## Refunds

A renewal can be refunded in full if the renewal order has not shipped yet. Once it has shipped, the normal Refund policy applies.

## Availability

Subscriptions are only available for US addresses. Promo codes cannot be combined with the subscription discount.

See also Cancel a subscription and Skip or pause a subscription.
