# Gooseneck kettle troubleshooting

## The kettle does not heat

Check that the kettle sits flat on its base and the base light turns on. Wipe the contacts under the kettle and on the base with a dry cloth.

## The display shows an error

Unplug the base for 30 seconds and plug it back in. An error that returns usually means a faulty heating element, which is covered by the Equipment warranty.

## White deposits inside

That is mineral scale. Fill the kettle with equal parts water and descaling solution, heat it, leave it for 15 minutes, then rinse well.
