# Flavored coffees and allergens

Our flavored coffees (such as Hazelnut, Vanilla and Cinnamon) are made by adding food-grade flavoring oils after roasting. They contain no sugar and no dairy.

Some flavorings may contain tree nut derivatives. All our coffee is roasted and packed in a facility that also handles flavored coffees, so we cannot guarantee any product is free from traces of nuts.

If you have a severe allergy, choose our unflavored single-origin coffees and contact us with any questions.
